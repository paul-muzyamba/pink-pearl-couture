Our Story

The Mwiinga
Store.

"The store that allows you to be in touch with your femininity."

Pink Pearl Couture ZM was born in Lusaka with one vision: to bring aspirational, fashion-forward womenswear to Zambian women who refuse to compromise on style. From our boutique floors in Northmead and Chilenje, we curate dresses for every occasion — a silk slip for a quiet evening, a sequin gown for the night you deserve.
